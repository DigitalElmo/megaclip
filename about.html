About & Safety

MegaClip is a hand-picked list of 670 games you can play straight in your browser. It's like a giant bookmarks page: every game runs on its maker's own website, and MegaClip just points you there.

What MegaClip does NOT do

  • No downloads. MegaClip never gives you a file to install or run.
  • No game files. Nothing is hosted here except this page and the little thumbnail pictures.
  • No ads, no cookies, no accounts, no logins, no tracking pixels.
  • We only count visits (Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you).

Don't take our word for it: check it yourself

  • Mozilla Observatory: the security grade for this site (it scores A+).
  • Google Safe Browsing: Google's check for unsafe sites.
  • VirusTotal: dozens of antivirus scanners at once.
  • The code on GitHub: every line of this site is public, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

How we keep the links healthy

  • Every link is checked by a robot once a week. Dead links, and links that suddenly send you to a different website, get fixed or removed.
  • Games open in a new tab, walled off from this page, so a game's site can't touch MegaClip.
  • Remember: once you click a game, you're on someone else's website. Never type a password or card number into a game you don't trust.

☕ Who pays for it?

  • MegaClip is free and has no ads. It's run by Digital Elmo, and kept going by people who chip in for a coffee. Support MegaClip on Ko-fi. Totally optional: every game stays free either way.
